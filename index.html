The Problem

Requests get lost between departments — and the blame lands on the wrong people

Hospitals lose capacity not from a shortage of beds alone, but from the absence of request tracking. When no one owns the request or its clock, delay and waste stay invisible until they reach the patient.

How It Works

Every request gets a route it cannot fall through

RAFEQ is an operational layer that sits on top of existing systems — read-only, aligned to the national FHIR standard, and outside medical-device classification (SaMD), so it deploys fast without disruption.

◉

A named owner

Every inter-department request has someone accountable. No request is left without a hand on it.

⏱

A target time

Each request carries a target derived automatically — never typed by hand — so the clock is always visible.

▲

Automatic escalation

If a request nears or breaches its target, it escalates on its own — delay is surfaced, not buried.

Why RAFEQ Is Different

Four answers to the four questions every hospital asks

Measures the request, not the patient. It tracks the workflow itself — seeing the bottleneck where it happens, without feeling like surveillance of staff.
Read-only, zero disruption. It runs on top of your systems without touching them — no risk to data or workflow.
Built on the national standard (FHIR). It speaks the language of NPHIES. Build once, connect to all — no rebuilding at every site.
Operational, outside SaMD. It does not diagnose or treat, so it avoids heavy regulatory approval and deploys quickly.
